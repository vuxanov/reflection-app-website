A system for understanding yourself.

VD combines structured journaling and guided self-reflection to help you explore your thoughts, navigate personal challenges, and uncover patterns across the cycles of your life.

Drawing on psychology, philosophy and esoteric traditions, it gives you different ways to understand yourself.

An independent project.

VD is developed by Dimitar Vuksanov, a product and UX designer.

Created out of an interest in psychology, philosophy and esoteric thought, VD brings these perspectives together in a thoughtfully designed space for self-reflection.

The idea is simple: to create a personal space for asking better questions, recognizing patterns, and understanding yourself over time.
