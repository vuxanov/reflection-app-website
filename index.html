Read the Hermetic principles. Work through a question that won’t leave you alone. Set an intention for the next few hours, or for the week ahead.

Old ideas. Something to try today.

The Seven Hermetic Principles are part of the app’s reading room. Mentalism, Correspondence, Polarity, Rhythm: ideas to read slowly, question, and bring back to your own experience.

Alongside them are practical exercises. Descent of the question asks why again, then again. Wheel of Becoming asks which part of your life needs attention. You can use the exercises without turning every thought into a conversation with AI.

An intention needs a timescale.

“What would you like to focus on in the next few hours?” That is one of the app’s questions. It asks for something different from a plan for the coming year.

The intentions follow four working rhythms: a few hours, a day, a week, and a lunar cycle. You can keep a short task in view without mistaking it for the whole direction of your life. The readings also look at seasons, including the Season of Awakening and The Quieting.

Private enough to be honest.

A useful reflection may be unfinished, contradictory, or difficult to explain. You should be able to write it without creating an account or sending it to a cloud AI service.

After downloading the model, conversations run locally on your phone. Your conversations and saved exercises are stored in the app. Device backups are a separate question; their behavior is being checked and is explained in the draft privacy page.

There is no feed to post to. There is no public profile to maintain. You decide whether to use the AI at all.
