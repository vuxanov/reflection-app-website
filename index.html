VD combines structured journaling and guided self-reflection to help you explore your thoughts, navigate personal challenges, and uncover patterns across the cycles of your life.

Structured journaling

Go beyond writing down your thoughts with guided exercises and frameworks for meaningful self-reflection.

Cycles of reflection

Reflect across hours, days, weeks, months, seasons and years. Set intentions for each cycle and return to saved reflections to notice recurring themes for yourself.

Multiple perspectives

Explore personal challenges through psychology, philosophy, symbolic thinking and esoteric traditions. Read about Hermetic principles and cycles at your own pace.

Your private, on-device guide

When you want another perspective, get guidance and questions from AI that runs on your iPhone. Your reflections aren’t sent to external AI servers.

AI is optional. Journaling, guided reflection, intention-setting and readings work without downloading a model.

An independent project.

VD is developed by Dimitar Vuksanov, a product and UX designer.

Created out of an interest in psychology, philosophy and esoteric thought, VD brings these perspectives together in a thoughtfully designed space for self-reflection.

The idea is simple: to create a personal space for asking better questions, recognizing patterns, and understanding yourself over time.
