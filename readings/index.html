Readings & practice.

A few pages from the ideas behind the app. You can read them here without installing anything.

The app puts Hermetic philosophy beside everyday questions about attention, habits and intention. These notes introduce that material and offer a place to begin. They aren’t predictions or instructions for how your life has to work.

From reading to an intention

Pick one question rather than trying to apply everything at once. Write down what you notice. If it points to an action, give that action a timescale: today, this week, or longer.

The app keeps the reading, guided exercises and intentions close together so you can return to the same thought from more than one direction.
