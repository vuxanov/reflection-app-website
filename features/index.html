The thinking behind cycles

A space that feels like yours.

Choose Elegant or Brutalist, then light or dark. You can also follow your iPhone’s system appearance. The structure stays familiar; the feel changes.

Change the accents. Choose the symbols.

Keep the theme’s default color or choose White, Emerald, Rose, Amber, Lavender, Rust or Blue. White becomes Ink in light mode. The accent carries through buttons, selections and borders.

Choose from Shri Yantra, Flower, Mountain, Geometry, Eye, Sun, Moon, Seven and Renewal, or keep the theme default. Eye, Sun, Moon and Renewal have both Elegant and Brutalist renderings. On iPhone, you can apply the chosen appearance as the app icon too.

About the ideas behind VD

Create a personal sigil

The Sigil exercise helps you distill an intention into words and letters for a symbol you draw yourself. It is a symbolic practice, rather than a promise of a particular outcome.

An on-device guide, when you want one.

Ask a question, talk through a challenge, or bring a completed reflection into a conversation. Replies are generated by a model on your iPhone; your words are not sent to a cloud AI service.

Journaling, guided reflection, intention-setting and readings work without downloading a model. AI chat requires an optional download of about 1.28 GB and enough storage and working memory. After setup, AI replies work offline.

You can adjust the AI’s style, response length and creativity in Settings. The app also supports typing and optional on-device dictation where available.

Read the privacy details

Your work, your choices.

No account, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Saved conversations stay on your device. Chats and saved guided conversations can be included in normal iPhone backups; downloaded models and other app records are excluded.

Delete saved app data and downloaded models from Settings. This does not erase older device backups. The model-download host receives technical network information during setup.

VD is a reflection tool, not a diagnosis or treatment service.
