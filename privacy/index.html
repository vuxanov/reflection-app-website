Privacy, plainly.

A draft explanation of how this iPhone app handles your information.

Draft updated 27 October 2026. This is not the final release policy. The app name, full publisher identity, backup behavior, and dependency review are still being finalized. This page is not yet suitable as the public policy URL for an App Store submission.

What you save

The app stores your name, conversations, intentions, exercise history, drafts, and preferences in its own storage on your phone. It also stores the downloaded AI model and information needed to find and load it.

Local AI conversations

The app sends prompts to the AI engine running on the phone. The current application code does not send conversation content to a cloud AI provider. A full dependency and network review is still in progress before final release claims are made.

The initial model download

The AI model is downloaded from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. Those services receive ordinary network request information, such as your IP address, when delivering the file. The download code does not include your chat messages in those requests.

Microphone and dictation

Dictation asks for microphone and speech-recognition permissions when you use it. The app requires on-device recognition and does not intentionally fall back to cloud transcription. Availability depends on your phone and recognition support. You can type instead and manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

Backups and deletion

Local processing and operating-system backups are separate. Backup behavior is under review: personal records appear to be excluded by the current storage-library default, while model files currently lack an explicit exclusion. The final policy will describe the verified behavior.

The app’s Delete data action removes saved app records and downloaded models from the current installation. It currently asks you to wait if a model download is running. Deleting current app data does not guarantee removal of copies in older operating-system backups.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, the information you choose to send is handled through email services to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive conversation content unnecessarily. Support retention and deletion procedures will be stated in the final policy.

This website

This static website includes no analytics scripts, advertising, collection forms, or external font requests. The hosting service may process technical request information to deliver the website. Host-specific details will be reviewed before public launch.

Questions

Contact Dimitar at dimitar@vuksanov.com with privacy or deletion questions. The complete public publisher identity and policy effective date will be supplied before release.
