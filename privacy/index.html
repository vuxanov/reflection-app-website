Privacy

Privacy information for VD.

The AI runs on your iPhone. Your chats aren’t sent to a cloud AI service.

Operated by Dimitar Vuksanov. Updated 28 October 2026.

What stays in the app

The app saves your display name, conversations, intentions, guided exercises, drafts and settings on your iPhone. You don’t need an account. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

iPhone backups

Guru chats and saved guided conversations are eligible for normal iPhone backups. Whether they are backed up depends on your Apple backup settings. The downloaded AI model, settings, drafts, intentions and other progress are excluded.

After restoring, the model will need to be downloaded again.

Model downloads

The AI model downloads from Hugging Face and its delivery services. These services receive network information, such as your IP address, and may retain technical logs for delivery, security and service improvement under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Their retention periods are controlled by those providers. Your conversations are not included in the download request. An internet connection is needed for the download; AI replies run on your phone afterward.

Dictation

The app’s built-in dictation asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission and requires on-device recognition. You can type instead. If you use dictation through your iPhone keyboard, its data handling depends on your keyboard and Apple settings.

Deleting data

Choose Delete data in the app’s Settings to remove its saved records and downloaded models from this iPhone. Finish any model download first. This does not erase older device backups or emails you have sent for support.

This website and support

This website has no analytics scripts, account system or forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical information needed to serve the pages.

If you email dimitar@vuksanov.com, your email address and message are handled by email services. Please don’t send private conversations unless they are needed to explain a problem.

Support emails are kept while a request is open and deleted within 90 days after it is resolved, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. This is a mailbox practice managed by Dimitar; the app does not automatically delete emails. Contact the same address to request access to or deletion of support information.
