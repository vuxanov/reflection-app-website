Privacy

The AI runs on your iPhone.

This is a draft. Backup behavior and the final privacy policy are still being checked.

Your data

The app stores your name, conversations, intentions, exercises, drafts, and settings on your phone. Conversations aren’t sent to a cloud AI service.

Downloads

The model downloads from Hugging Face. The download service receives network information such as your IP address. Your chats aren’t part of that download request.

Dictation

Dictation needs microphone and speech permission. It requires on-device recognition. You can type instead.

Deleting data

Delete data in Settings removes your app records and downloaded models. Finish any active download first.

Device backups are separate. We’re checking what gets backed up. Deleting data in the app doesn’t erase older backups.

This website and support

This website has no analytics scripts or forms. GitHub may keep technical logs to serve it. If you email me, your message is handled by email services.
