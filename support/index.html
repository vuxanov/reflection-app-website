Quick help

The model won’t download.

Check your connection and free storage, then retry.

Dictation isn’t working.

Check microphone and speech permissions. On-device recognition also needs to be available. You can type instead.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete data. Finish any active model download first.

When can I download the app?

It’s still in development. There’s no release date yet. The first version will be free, with no in-app purchases.
