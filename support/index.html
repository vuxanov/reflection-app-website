A few useful answers.

Why does the app need a download?

The AI model is a separate, large file downloaded before your first AI conversation. Use a reliable connection and leave enough free storage. Once the model is downloaded and loaded, AI inference runs locally.

What if the model download fails?

Check your internet connection and free storage, then retry. The app checks the recommended download before using it. If the problem continues, email the error message and your iPhone details.

Which iPhones will be supported?

The final supported device and iOS range is still being tested. This page will be updated with verified requirements before launch. The app is being prepared for iPhone, not as a dedicated iPad app.

Why is dictation unavailable?

It needs microphone and speech-recognition permission and on-device recognition support. Check the app’s permissions in iPhone Settings. You can continue by typing if dictation is unavailable.

How do I delete saved data?

Use Delete data in the app’s Settings. It removes saved app records and downloaded models from the current installation. Finish any active model download first. The final privacy policy will explain backup and restore limitations.

Is the app free?

Version 1 is planned as a free app with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It is still in development; no App Store release date has been announced.
